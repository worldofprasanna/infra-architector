# Infrastructure Evaluator

1. Why we want to create this?
We want to have a lead magnet to pull potential customers to us. This should able to showcase our ability to ask the right question and look pleasingly good, as this is the first entrypoint for our customers

2. What are the key points to be noted?
- Ask just 10 Business questions and once they finish it, capture their business email and store it in our DB
- Give them the audit of their Infrastructure in PDF file and it should highlight the potential gaps
- Should be hosted in evaluator.ezyinfra.dev domain
- Enable Analytics, so we know how many people have visited the website and how many dropped while giving their business email
- Put a video on the home page so that users will feel confident that it works
- At the last there should be a schedule call with Prasanna & analytics should be enabled to see how many people actually schedule the call
- In the home page show the no of people who used the evaluator tool
- Prepare a very small PDF explaining the Business question and how to acheive it in the AWS Cloud. This download link will be available for those who give their business email

3. Examples of Business questions:

- Can a developer accidentally delete production data? If so, when & how will you know about it?
- If I ask you, who accessed production ec2 instance at 3am EST time? do you have an answer for it?
- Do you have visibility of a single request going across all your services?
- Is your infrastructure documented outside of people's heads?
- When was your last production outage? why it happened and how confident are you that the same issue will not occur again?

Prasanna will prepare more such real world questions & what services to use to avoid that as a PDF.
